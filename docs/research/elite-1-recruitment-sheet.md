# ELITE-1 recruitment sheet (owner)

One page for Saleem. Everything needed to fill 10 seats and nothing else. The
full instrument is `docs/research/elite-1-instrument-en.md` and
`docs/research/elite-1-instrument-ar.md`; this sheet does not repeat it, and the
facilitator reads the instrument rather than this file.

No participant is contacted until the owner authorises recruitment. Codex has
authorised preparation and recruitment; the round itself is measured from
consented research notes and manual observation, and no product telemetry runs.

## The 10 seats

5 supply side, 5 demand side. Fill by seat, not by whoever answers first: the
mix is what makes the round readable, and 5 landlords who all work in English and
all manage one asset is a round that answers one question 5 times.

| Seat | Side | Who this person is | The seat exists to test | Language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Supply | Landlord or owner who sets the asking terms on their own space, single asset | Whether a first-time lister can register an organisation and publish alone | Arabic |
| S2 | Supply | Landlord or owner who sets terms, has never listed on any online platform | Whether the product teaches itself to somebody with no portal habits | Arabic |
| S3 | Supply | Asset or portfolio manager responsible for occupancy across more than 3 assets | Whether inventory management works above one listing | Either |
| S4 | Supply | Leasing administrator who prepares listing material in-house | Whether the person who actually types the record can complete it | Either |
| S5 | Supply | Leasing administrator at a managing agent, acting for owners | Whether acting on behalf of another party is even expressible today | Either |
| D1 | Demand | Expansion or network manager for a retail, food or service brand, currently in an open search | Whether a live searcher finds a real candidate | Arabic |
| D2 | Demand | Acquisition or real-estate manager inside a company occupying its own space, currently in an open search | Whether requirement creation matches how an occupier states need | Arabic |
| D3 | Demand | Tenant representation or leasing decision maker acting for an occupier | Whether an intermediary trusts the evidence enough to pass it on | Either |
| D4 | Demand | Any qualifying demand-side role whose search covers more than one city | Whether search and matching hold up across cities | Either |
| D5 | Demand | Any qualifying demand-side role who used a Saudi listing portal in the last 12 months | What the product is compared against, in their words | Either |

Minimum thresholds the mix must hit, checked before the first session: at least 4
of the 10 work primarily in Arabic; at least 2 supply-side participants decide
terms themselves; at least 2 supply-side participants prepare the listing
material themselves; at least 2 demand-side participants are in an open search
now. If a seat cannot be filled, run 9 and record the gap rather than
substituting a weaker profile into it.

## Exclusion criteria

Do not invite, and disqualify at screening: anyone employed by, invested in or
advising SAT; anyone whose only real-estate experience is residential; anyone who
cannot describe one specific property or one specific search in the screener,
because every task depends on having a real frame of reference to compare
against; anyone whose participation requires a signed confidentiality agreement,
until the owner has approved one; and anyone whose commercial relationship with
Saleem would make an honest negative answer awkward, which is a judgement call
and is worth making strictly, because a polite session produces nothing.

## Time commitment

45 minutes for the session itself, on a video call, screen shared. Plus a
5 minute screener call or message beforehand. Nothing to prepare, nothing to read
in advance, no follow-up obligation. Tell people 45 minutes and hold to it; a
session that overruns teaches the next person's calendar to decline.

Owner time, for planning: about 12 hours total across the round, being 10 sessions
of 45 minutes plus scheduling, plus write-up time between sessions.

## Language

Ask, do not assume. Screener question 5 is the one that decides it: which
language they work in for property matters, and which they prefer for the
session. The session runs entirely in the chosen language and the product runs in
the same one, right to left throughout for an Arabic session. Seats S1, S2, D1
and D2 are held for Arabic so the round cannot quietly become an English round.

## Invitation message

Short version, for WhatsApp or a first message. The longer email version is
section 2 of the instrument.

> I am building SAT Markets, an early and not yet public platform for Saudi
> commercial real estate. Before building anything further I want to watch a few
> people who actually do this work try to use it. Could I borrow 45 minutes on a
> video call? You would use it on your own screen and try a few ordinary tasks
> while I stay quiet and watch. Nothing to prepare, nothing to buy or sign, and
> you do not need to share anything confidential; I will give you a fictional
> example to work from. If you are willing, send me two or three times that suit
> you in the next two weeks.

Arabic version, same message, sent as written:

> وقتين أو ثلاثة تناسبك خلال الأسبوعين القادمين.

Do not describe the product as launched, verified at scale, or as having users.
It has 50 preview listings, 6 requirements and 0 registered interests, and an
invitation that overstates that is the first unsupported claim of the round.

## What each participant needs

Every participant, all 10: a laptop or desktop with a current browser, a working
microphone, and the ability to share their screen on the call. The session runs
on the deployed preview, which is not indexed and carries the preview banner.
Send the preview link only at the start of the session, not with the invitation.

Supply side, S1 to S5: a test account on the preview, named `partner-s1` to
`partner-s5`, belonging to a fictional organisation created for the round. Two of
the five register live during the session as task S1; decide which two before the
round and record it, because a participant who registers live and one who starts
signed in are not running the same task and their times do not compare. The owner
sets the passwords and gives them at the start of the session. No participant is
ever asked to reuse a password of their own.

Demand side, D1 to D5: a test account named `partner-d1` to `partner-d5`. No
organisation setup needed.

Nobody needs a phone, a physical device, an app install, or anything from their
own systems. If a participant can only join from a phone, record it and run the
session anyway; a mobile session is worth more than a cancelled one, and the
observation sheet has a device column for exactly this reason.

## Scheduling

Offer a 45 minute slot and book 60, so the session is never rushed by the next
meeting. Space sessions at least 90 minutes apart, and run no more than 3 in a
day, because the write-up between sessions is where the round's value is
recorded and a facilitator on their fourth session stops noticing things.

Run the two supply-side Arabic sessions early, S1 and S2, because they exercise
registration and Arabic direction together, which is where the round is most
likely to hit something that needs fixing before the remaining 8 people see it.

Before the first session, confirm the preview data checks in section 5 of the
instrument: at least 20 published listings with at least 8 in Riyadh, at least 4
with more than one photograph, at least 2 with a real Evidence Passport record,
at least 1 with a visible gap, `/rent-index` loading with its REGA Rental Index
(Ejar) attribution in both languages, and `/requirements` with at least 2
published requirements. If a check fails, fix it before the round rather than
explaining it during a session.

Reconfirm 24 hours ahead. Expect 2 or 3 no-shows across 10 seats and over-invite
accordingly rather than compressing the mix later.

## Consent steps

Four moments, in order, none of them skippable.

At invitation: state plainly that there is nothing to buy, sign or agree to, that
nothing confidential is needed, and that a recording will be requested but is
optional. Nobody should arrive surprised by any of it.

At the start of the session: read section 3 of the instrument aloud, without
paraphrasing the recording paragraph or the confidentiality paragraph. Ask for
recording permission explicitly and wait for a clear yes or no. Attending is not
consent to being recorded, and being recorded is not consent to being quoted by
name. If the answer is no, take notes instead and record the refusal.

During the session: if a participant volunteers something confidential, an actual
rent, a tenant name, an expansion plan or a contract date, do not write it down,
do not enter it into the product, and do not repeat it in the findings. Note only
that a confidential detail was mentioned, and continue.

At the close: read the closing script. Confirm the recording is not published,
offer deletion within the following week, and ask whether there is anything they
would rather not have used. Honour that without asking why.

Written consent is not required for this round because nothing personal is
retained beyond the session record, which holds side, role group, city, portfolio
or search scope, language, prior platform use and device, and no name, employer,
phone number or email. The recruitment list with real contact details stays with
the owner and never enters this repository. If the owner later wants written
consent forms, that is an O5 counsel item and does not block the round from
running on the spoken script.
